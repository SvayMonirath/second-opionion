# /second-opinion-product — Product and UX Shortcut

## Purpose

Examine whether a product direction solves a meaningful problem and whether the proposed experience is usable, feasible and appropriately scoped.

## Method

1. Identify target users, context, problem and intended outcome.
2. Separate user-provided facts, research evidence, assumptions and product hypotheses.
3. Examine problem severity, frequency, existing workarounds and unmet needs where evidence exists.
4. Evaluate desirability, usability, accessibility, feasibility, viability, scope and differentiation as appropriate.
5. Inspect interaction flow, cognitive burden, error recovery, onboarding and user control.
6. Challenge feature bloat and distinguish essential value from speculative additions.
7. Compare alternatives and identify the smallest test that could reduce uncertainty.
8. Do not invent interviews, analytics, customer quotes, market size or product behavior.

## Collaboration guidance

Participants examine the same product proposition from different analytical roles. Do not create a simulated research panel or claim user validation from agent opinions. Existing product-research and UX skills should be reused.

## Report

State the user problem, evidence, assumptions, strongest product risks, experience issues, feasible scope, alternatives and validation needed.
