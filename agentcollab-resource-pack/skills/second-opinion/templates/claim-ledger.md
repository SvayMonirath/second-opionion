## Status definitions
- VERIFIED — direct check confirmed within recorded conditions/scope.
- SUPPORTED — reliable evidence supports it; not directly reproduced in target conditions.
- INFERRED — reasonable derivation from available evidence.
- ASSUMPTION — provisional and not checked.
- UNVERIFIED — insufficient evidence or access.
- DISPUTED — credible evidence/reasoning conflicts and remains unresolved.
- REFUTED — adequate evidence/check contradicts it as stated.

Agreement among participants is not a claim status.
