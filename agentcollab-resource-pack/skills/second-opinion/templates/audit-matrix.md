## Result labels
- **PASS:** objective evidence demonstrates the acceptance condition.
- **PARTIAL:** some, but not all, of the acceptance condition is met.
- **FAIL:** evidence demonstrates the condition is not met.
- **NOT VERIFIED:** not enough evidence or no actual check was performed.
- **BLOCKED:** the check could not be completed because of an external dependency, permission or capability.
- **NOT APPLICABLE:** the requirement does not apply, with reason.

A plan, design note or self-reported completion is not proof that an implementation requirement passed.
