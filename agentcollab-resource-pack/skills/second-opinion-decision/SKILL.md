# /second-opinion-decision — Decision Analysis Shortcut

## Purpose

Help the decision owner choose among options using explicit criteria, evidence, constraints and trade-offs. The AI advises; it does not assume decision authority.

## Method

1. State the decision, owner, deadline and consequence of error.
2. Establish hard constraints and must-have criteria before selecting a preferred option.
3. Identify viable alternatives, including the status quo and deferral when relevant.
4. Gather evidence for each option and label assumptions.
5. Compare cost, benefit, feasibility, dependencies, reversibility, implementation burden, risk and uncertainty.
6. Challenge the leading option and identify conditions under which alternatives are better.
7. Avoid false precision in weighted scoring; explain qualitative judgments.
8. Identify the smallest additional evidence that could change the decision.
9. Recommend one option, a conditional choice or further investigation.

## Collaboration guidance

Independent participants should assess the same options against the same criteria. Do not use participant votes as the decision rule. Preserve material minority reasoning. Use existing domain-specific skills for technical or product facts.

## Report

Include decision statement, criteria, options, evidence, trade-offs, sensitivity/conditions, recommendation, reversibility and unresolved uncertainty.
