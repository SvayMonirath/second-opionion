# Challenge Packet

**Challenge ID:**  
**Target claim/conclusion:**  
**Original participant/claim source:**  

## Challenge
- Why may the claim be wrong, incomplete or conditional?
- What evidence, counterexample or logical defect supports the challenge?
- What would be the impact if the challenge is correct?
- What specific verification would resolve it?

## Response requested
- Accept / partially accept / reject / unresolved.
- Explain why.
- Identify any new evidence needed.

## Challenge quality check
- Is the target claim precise?
- Is the concern material to the assignment?
- Does the challenge rely on evidence or a clearly labelled hypothesis?
- Does it avoid manufacturing disagreement?
