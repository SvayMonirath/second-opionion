## 1. Interpretation
Restate the assignment and evaluation criteria without adding a preferred conclusion.

## 2. Initial conclusion
State the best-supported answer or candidate solution at this stage. It is acceptable to say the evidence is insufficient.

## 3. Main claims and rationale
List atomic claims, concise reasoning and evidence. Reference source/evidence IDs where possible.

## 4. Assumptions
Identify assumptions and explain their impact.

## 5. Alternative explanation or solution
Identify a genuinely plausible alternative. Do not manufacture a difference if none is material.

## 6. Potential failure modes and counterexamples
Identify the strongest concerns and how they could be checked.

## 7. Evidence that would change the conclusion
Specify concrete observations, sources, tests or conditions.

## 8. Uncertainty and limitations
State what was not checked, unavailable tools and relevant blind spots.

## 9. Scope statement
Confirm which materials and tools were actually examined. Do not claim unperformed work.
